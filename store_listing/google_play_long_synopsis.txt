Explore grammar in context with Grammar Study, an offline-first grammar reference and practice app for learners and teachers.

Browse grammar categories and definitions, find vocabulary in example sentences, compare plain and tagged forms, and practise with three games. Study content is bundled with the app, so the reference and games work without an internet connection.

FIND GRAMMAR IN REAL SENTENCES

The Grammar tab groups vocabulary by category. Choose a category to see its definition and values, search as you type, and select a value to open sentences that use it. Each matching sentence can be switched between plain text and its tagged grammatical structure.

The Structure tab presents tagged sentence examples; Sentence presents the same collection without tags. Both lists filter as you type and rank entries that begin with your search first.

PRACTICE WITH THREE GAMES

CROSSWORD
Solve generated crosswords from grammar vocabulary. Clues show direction, answer length, and word type. Select a numbered cell and enter the active word one letter at a time; at a crossing, choose which clue to answer. Completed words show correct and incorrect letters in color. A running Correct/Incorrect score counts each entered letter once when a clue is first completed; revealed clues do not score. Totals continue across new puzzles while the game screen remains active. Open a clue or select its active cell again for a sentence hint and an option to reveal that word. Hint shows masked puzzle answers and word types. Clear Cell clears editable letters in the active word; New Puzzle starts another grid.

SLIDE
Move the vertical letter columns to line up the hidden answer with the target row, then select Submit. See how many letters are correct; running Correct/Incorrect totals count every position on each submission. Correct positions lock while the remaining columns stay movable. Select the word type to read its definition, use Hint for a sentence containing the answer, or choose New Word.

DRAG
Rebuild a tagged sentence by dragging each word from the word list into its matching blank. A running Correct/Incorrect score counts each correct placement and each incorrect drop on a word target. A drop outside a target does not count. Matching colors confirm correct placements and fix them in place; incorrect drops flash red and leave words available to try again. Select New Sentence for another round.

STUDY YOUR WAY

- Search grammar values, tagged structures, and plain sentences
- Switch matching sentences between plain and tagged forms
- Read available definitions in Grammar and in Slide
- Choose System, Light, or Dark theme
- Resume from the grammar category you used last
- Track running correct and incorrect values; Crossword counts each clue once per puzzle, and scores are not saved between sessions
- Start another round at any time; game progress is not saved between sessions
- Work on phones, tablets, and larger screens
- Check loaded data counts and restore the bundled database

SUPPORT AND PRIVACY

Use Contact Us in the Information tab to send a question, correction, or suggestion. The form sends your name, return email, message, and app diagnostics, including package, app version and build, platform, layout, orientation, and submission time. Submissions loads a remote CSV and displays entries associated with this app. Sending a message and loading Submissions require an internet connection; reference and game content do not. The app includes no advertising or analytics.

Grammar reference content ships with the app for offline use. Your theme and selected grammar category are saved on your device; game rounds and scores are not saved between sessions. On web, saved preferences depend on browser storage.
