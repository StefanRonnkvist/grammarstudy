See how words work in context with Grammar Study, a grammar reference and practice app for students, teachers, and independent learners.

Look up a grammar term, read the definition, compare the same sentence plain and annotated, then practise it in one of three games. All study content is bundled, so the app works without a continuous internet connection.

FIND GRAMMAR IN REAL SENTENCES

The Grammar tab groups vocabulary into categories. Pick a category to see its definition and its values, search within it, then select a value to open the sentences that use it. Every result starts as plain text and switches to its tagged grammatical structure when selected.

The Structure tab lists the same sentences with their grammar tags, so you can study annotated patterns. The Sentence tab lists them as clean, readable examples. Both filter and sort as you type, and matches beginning with your search are listed first.

PRACTICE WITH THREE GAMES

CROSSWORD
Solve generated crosswords built from grammar vocabulary. Clues give the direction, length, and word type. Select a cell and type one letter at a time: correct entries turn green, incorrect ones turn red. Select a clue, or tap the same cell twice, for a sentence hint and the option to reveal that word. Hint summarizes the whole puzzle, Clear Cell removes the selected entry, and New Puzzle starts a fresh grid.

SLIDE
Drag each vertical letter column until one letter lines up with the horizontal target row, then select Submit. Correct positions lock in place while the remaining columns stay movable. Select the underlined word type for its definition, use Hint for a sentence containing the answer, or select New Word for another round.

DRAG
Rebuild a tagged sentence by dragging each word from the word list into its matching blank. Matching colors confirm a correct placement; an incorrect drop flashes red and leaves the word available to try again. Select New Sentence for another round.

STUDY YOUR WAY

- Search grammar values, tagged structures, and plain sentences
- Switch each result between its natural and annotated form
- Open a word type for its definition without leaving the puzzle
- Choose System, Light, or Dark theme
- Resume from the grammar category you used last
- Work on phones, tablets, and larger screens
- Check loaded data counts and restore the bundled database

SUPPORT AND PRIVACY

Use Contact Us in the Information tab to send a question or correction, and Submissions to review entries for this app. Sending a message and loading Submissions require an internet connection; reference and game content do not. The app includes no advertising and no analytics.

Grammar reference content ships with the app for offline use. Your theme and selected grammar category are saved on your device; game rounds are not saved between sessions. On web, saved preferences depend on browser storage.
